About me

Hello, my name is Mohammed Wissam, I live in Baghdad, and I love technology and programming.

This passion began when I was 13 years old. I was playing Minecraft PE and using Command Blocks to create my worlds. Later, I switched to game development because of the freedom it offers.

I started with the Unity game engine. But because of my hardware limitations, I moved to libraries like SDL2 and frameworks like LÖVE. Through these tools, I learned programming concepts, and was able to focus more on the code.

And these limitations made me explore the world of Unix-like systems and open-source software. Over time, I realized that I love learning how systems and devices work.

Today, I use Void Linux, and I am trying to improve my knowledge of Lua and C. I also enjoy creating simple projects. This website is one of them. I created it as a public archive of what I learn and try from time to time.
